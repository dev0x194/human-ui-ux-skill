What feels “AI-generated” on the left?

Vague outcome claims, a gradient headline, invented-looking dashboard metrics, interchangeable feature cards, a familiar purple CTA, and little evidence of the user's actual day-to-day problem. The visual finish is fine; the product thinking is thin.

Generic copyDecorative dashboardPattern repetition

Why the right side feels more intentional

It opens with a concrete promise, uses an editorial visual identity, gives the product itself room to prove the value, and lets you complete a real micro-interaction. The hierarchy, visual rhythm, and restrained colors support the story.

Distinct brand voiceFunctional demoClear hierarchy
Takeaway: Better design is not simply fewer gradients. It starts with a specific user, a specific promise, and evidence in the interface.Try the view controls, task checkboxes, and CTA buttons.
